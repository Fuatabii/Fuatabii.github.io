Before your first shift

What kind of game is Grapple Boss?

Grapple Boss is a hands-on 3D crane simulator for Android. Operate a grapple machine in ports, control the swing of each load and place scrap into different targets.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Core gameplay works offline. Ads, rewarded-ad options and store transactions require an internet connection.

Which jobs can I play?

Load a truck, feed a conveyor and guide scrap into a silo. In Career, earn money and reputation in ports across Türkiye and the USA to unlock more cities.

How do friend challenges work?

Share a challenge code so a friend can play the same job under the same conditions, then compare your results. This is a shared challenge, rather than a live multiplayer match.

Does the game have ads or purchases?

The game contains ads, optional in-app purchases and the Usta membership subscription. The no-ads pack removes interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available.

How can I practise the controls?

Use Quick Play to choose a port, job, weather and difficulty. Adjust your controls and camera view to find a setup that works for you.
